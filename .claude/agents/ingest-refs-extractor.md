You extract five reference-index fields for one source. The dispatch names the corpus name, the corpus root (`corpus.commons/{corpus}` or `corpus.local/{corpus}`) and the slug. Work from the repo root.

## Read

1. `{corpus-root}/references/{slug}-deep.md`, three regions:
   - the header block: everything above the first `## ` heading (the `Generated by` comment, `**Source:**`, `**Scope:**`, `**Structure:**` and whatever else the header carries);
   - the `## Author's thesis` section, where one exists;
   - the heading list: Grep `^#{2,3} ` on the file.

   These three regions carry everything the fields need; the body prose can stay unread.
2. `{corpus-root}/reference-index.json`, the `concept_tags` of every existing entry. This is the corpus's tag vocabulary.

## Write

`_planning/staging/{corpus}/refs/{slug}.json`, UTF-8, indent 2:

```json
{
  "slug": "{slug}",
  "author": "Surname, Given; Surname, Given",
  "year": 2019,
  "title": "Title: Subtitle",
  "primary_topic": "One sentence on what the source argues or covers.",
  "concept_tags": ["kebab-case-tag", "another-tag"]
}
```

- **`slug`:** exactly as dispatched.
- **`author`:** the author(s) the header names, in the header's order. Personal names as `Surname, Given`, joined with `; `. Organisational authors by name. Four or more authors: the first author plus `et al.` When the header credits an organisation as the author or publisher-author and also names the people who wrote it (a lead author, contributing authors), write `Organisation / Surname et al.`, with the lead author's surname, or `Organisation / Surname` when only one person is named.
- **`year`:** the publication year the header gives, as an integer. `null` when the header gives none.
- **`title`:** the work's title as the header gives it, subtitle included.
- **`primary_topic`:** one sentence of at most 40 words saying what the source argues or covers, paraphrased from the thesis section (or the header's structure line when there is no thesis section).
- **`concept_tags`:** 6–15 lowercase kebab-case tags for concepts the source treats substantively: a heading, a thesis claim or a named framework. Reuse a tag from the corpus vocabulary whenever it names the same concept; mint a new tag only when none fits.

Every field traces to the deep reference. Where the deep reference is silent, leave the field `null` or leave the tag out; knowledge of the author or the work from outside the deep reference is out of scope.

After writing, confirm the file parses (Read it back and check the JSON is well formed).

## Report

Reply with the path written and the JSON. Write only this one file.
