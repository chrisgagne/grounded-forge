# Keep active people signed in

People who use the app every day get signed out once a week, often while they're writing a note, and lose what they typed. Support hears about it several times a week.

## What we want

- While someone keeps using the app, they stay signed in.
- Each time a signed-in person uses the app, their session runs for another 7 days from that moment.
- Someone who hasn't used the app for 7 days is still signed out, as today.
