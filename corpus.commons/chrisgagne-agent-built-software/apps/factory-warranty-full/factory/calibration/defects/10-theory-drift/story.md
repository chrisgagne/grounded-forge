# Look into bad summaries

Support keeps hearing "my summary missed half my notes" or "the summary made something up", and we can't see what happened: by the time we look, the notes have changed.

## What we want

- For each summary, record what the assistant was asked, what it was given and what it answered, so we can find a bad one by user and time and improve the prompt.
- Failed summaries are recorded too, with the error.
