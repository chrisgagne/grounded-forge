Image-only content, inspected on a page render and not in the converted text: the NHS Improvement logo; eight supporter logos under "Supported by:" (Academy of Medical Royal Colleges, AvMA, Care Quality Commission, General Medical Council, National Guardian Freedom to Speak Up, Nursing & Midwifery Council, NHS England, Royal College of Nursing); and a values strapline (collaboration, trust, respect, innovation, courage, compassion).

The guide is attributed on the poster to "the work of Professor James Reason and the National Patient Safety Agency's Incident Decision Tree". It is a one-page decision aid that supports a conversation between managers about whether a staff member involved in a patient safety incident requires specific individual support or intervention to work safely. Every recommendation keeps the wider investigation or the wider actions it indicates. The poster's working assumption—stated explicitly in the preamble—is that "action singling out an individual is rarely appropriate" and that most patient-safety issues have "deeper causes" requiring "wider action".

The poster's footer credits `improvement.nhs.uk`, which now redirects to england.nhs.uk; NHS England's patient-safety pages describe the 2018 guide as its own and say the being fair tool replaces it (both checked 2026-10-17). The PDF retrieved for ingestion is the copy hosted at cpcw.org.uk.
