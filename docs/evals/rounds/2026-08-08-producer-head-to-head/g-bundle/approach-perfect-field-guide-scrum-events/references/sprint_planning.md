# Definition

Sprint Planning is the event in which the Scrum Team identifies a Sprint Goal, forecasts which Increment it can deliver, and jointly plans how to complete the selected work.

# Time-box

- Hold Sprint Planning at the beginning of the Sprint.
- Allow roughly one to two hours per week of Sprint duration.
- Do not exceed eight hours for a one-month Sprint.

# Participants

- **Required:** the entire Scrum Team.
- **Invited:** stakeholders and subject-matter experts selected to support the team. Guests may support the team but must not interfere with its process.

# Inputs

- A healthy Product Backlog with roughly 1½ to two Sprints of prioritized items that are mostly “Ready.”
- The latest product Increment.
- The Development Team’s likely capacity, including expected leave.
- Historical performance, sometimes measured as Velocity in Story Points.

# Outputs

- A prioritized Sprint Backlog that the team forecasts it can complete within the Sprint.
- A Sprint Goal that can be achieved through at least most of the Sprint Backlog.
- An initial plan for how the Development Team will deliver the Increment.

# Sample three-hour agenda

1. Check in socially and address incomplete items from the preceding Sprint.
2. Identify available capacity and use historical performance to establish a realistic forecast.
3. Agree on a Sprint Goal.
4. Decide what can be done by discussing candidate Product Backlog items and selecting an amount of work determined by the Development Team.
5. Take a break.
6. Decide how the work will be completed through high-level design and, where useful, tasks small enough to complete in about a day.

# Operating guidance

- Capture outcomes from the customer’s perspective and leave implementation choices to the Development Team.
- Renegotiate selected items with the Product Owner if planning reveals that the Sprint Goal or Sprint Backlog is unrealistic.
- Treat the Sprint Goal and Sprint Backlog as a good-faith forecast and focusing tool, not a guarantee.
- If planning the work cannot fit in the time-box, improve Product Owner preparation and Backlog Refinement.
