# Definition

The Sprint Review is the event in which the Scrum Team and stakeholders inspect the completed Increment, exchange knowledge, and adapt the Product Backlog.

# Goals

- Elicit feedback on the Increment from stakeholders and, ideally, end users.
- Improve the Product Backlog collaboratively.
- Build transparency and trust between the Scrum Team and stakeholders.

# Time-box

- Allow approximately 15 to 60 minutes per week of Sprint duration.
- One hour is suggested for a two-week Sprint.
- Do not exceed four hours for a one-month Sprint.

# Participants

- **Required:** the Scrum Team.
- **Strongly suggested:** key stakeholders and end users invited by the Product Owner.

# Input

- The updated Sprint Backlog and knowledge of whether the Sprint Goal was met.

# Output

- An updated Product Backlog reflecting stakeholder feedback and new knowledge about factors such as the market, competition, and available resources.

# Sample one-hour agenda

1. The Product Owner states whether the Sprint Goal was met and identifies which Sprint Backlog items are and are not “Done.”
2. The Development Team briefly describes notable successes, difficulties, and blockers.
3. The Development Team demonstrates “Done” items, solicits critical feedback, and answers questions.
4. The Product Owner describes the Product Backlog’s current state and may offer scope or date forecasts.
5. Stakeholders share relevant changes in the market and possible product uses.
6. Review timing, resources, possible features, and distribution channels for the next release.
7. Decide collaboratively what to do next and how to prepare the Product Backlog for Sprint Planning.

# Operating guidance

- Treat the Sprint Review as a working feedback and adaptation session, not a status meeting or one-sided demonstration.
- Demonstrate only work that meets the Definition of “Done.”
