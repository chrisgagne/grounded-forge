# Results-chain terms

- **Inputs:** The resources, broadly defined, used or required by an intervention.
- **Activities:** The intervention’s actions, events, and undertakings.
- **Outputs:** The intervention’s immediate tangible products, often comparable to project deliverables.
- **Outcomes:** The potential short- and medium-term effects of the intervention; these are generally less tangible and harder to measure than outputs.
- **Impacts:** The intervention’s long-term effects or the long-term changes to which it contributes.

# Causal reading

A Theory of Change diagram connects an intervention to its outcomes through causal pathways. The categories usually flow from inputs and activities through outputs and outcomes to impacts, although diagrams vary in detail and layout.

Key assumptions about how each step will be realised should be made explicit. A Theory of Change is a theory to be tested and refined, not a map of reality.
