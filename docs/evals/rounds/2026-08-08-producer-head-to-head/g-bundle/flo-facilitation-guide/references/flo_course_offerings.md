# FLO Course Offerings

Facilitating Learning Online (FLO) is a family of facilitated, participatory online courses for developing online facilitation and learning-design skills.

| Offering | Primary focus | Mode and length | Typical participant time | Typical facilitator time | Assessment approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FLO Fundamentals | Adult and online learning concepts, community building, and online facilitation strategies | Mostly asynchronous over five consecutive weeks, with two or three optional but recommended one-hour synchronous sessions | 6–12 hours per week; more during the participant's team-facilitation week | 15–25 hours per week during preparation and delivery; first-time hosts may need more | Learning journals, self-assessment rubrics, constructive peer feedback, and reflection on team facilitation |
| FLO Design | Designing effective online learning spaces, short learning units, and prototype activities | Facilitated asynchronous and synchronous learning over four consecutive weeks | Approximately 6–8 hours per week; more for participants new to online teaching and learning or preparing a final showcase | Approximately 15 hours per week during delivery, potentially more in Weeks 1 and 4; preparation time varies | A self-assessment rubric, a selected quality framework, weekly Studio feedback, and a final reflective activity |
| FLO Synchronous | Planning, facilitating, and following up after synchronous online learning sessions | A facilitated asynchronous course with mandatory and optional synchronous sessions over three consecutive weeks | Approximately 6–8 hours per week; more for Practicing Facilitators or participants with little prior experience | Approximately 15 hours per week, with possible additional time for participant-led sessions in the final week | Track-specific self-assessment, feedback on Practicing Facilitator sessions, and reflection on synchronous facilitation |
| FLO MicroCourses | A single practical topic that produces a draft or prototype through research, creation, review, and feedback | Facilitated asynchronous learning over five days | Approximately 5 hours in total; more for participants new to online teaching and learning | At least 5 hours during the week, depending on experience, enrolment, and the amount of direct feedback provided | Peer review guided by a feedback rubric; no fixed completion requirements |

## Distinguishing features

- **FLO Fundamentals** alternates participants between learner and facilitator roles and organizes the course around five weekly topics: Building Learning Community, Diversity of Learners, Responsive Facilitation, Collaboration, and Reflective Practice.
- **FLO Design** uses weekly collaborative Studio forums in which participants share evolving designs and receive developmental feedback.
- **FLO Synchronous** offers two participant tracks: Reviewing Participant and Practicing Facilitator.
- **FLO MicroCourses** use a five-step activity packet: investigate the topic, draft an artifact, post it, review peers' work, and exchange feedback.
