# FLO Synchronous Participant Tracks

FLO Synchronous participants select one of two tracks by the end of Week 1.

| Track | Definition | Intended learning outcomes | Track-specific completion expectations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reviewing Participant | Reviews and discusses effective synchronous online facilitation and provides feedback to people practising those skills. | Identify effective planning and facilitation strategies; provide effective feedback to synchronous facilitators; explain the importance of evaluating synchronous sessions. | Participate in at least two Practicing Facilitator sessions and provide effective feedback for at least two sessions. |
| Practicing Facilitator | Plans and leads a short synchronous online event, then reflects on the experience and receives feedback. | Apply effective design strategies; lead a session with appropriate facilitation skills; use a web-based platform at a basic level; use reflection and feedback to evaluate a session. | Lead a session of at least 15 minutes using many of the skills in the Facilitation Session Guide; participate in at least one other Practicing Facilitator session; provide effective feedback for at least one session; reflect using the 3-2-1 structure and invite at least one piece of feedback. |

## Expectations shared by both tracks

To meet the common course requirements, a participant should:

- Participate in at least two of the three synchronous sessions led by the course facilitators.
- Make at least one meaningful original contribution and one meaningful reply in each weekly discussion or activity.
- Complete the weekly activities and submit the track-specific self-assessment rubric at the end of the course.

The two-track model allows participants to match the level of practical facilitation risk to their current experience while remaining active contributors to the course.
