Software engineering is the collection of activities performed by people directly involved in producing and maintaining software.

Its traditional activity set includes:

- obtaining requirements;
- creating specifications;
- designing at every level;
- writing and maintaining code;
- writing manuals;
- fixing problems; and
- providing user support.

Organizational size changes who performs these activities. Large organizations tend to compartmentalize them, while people in small companies may participate in the full path from an initial sales inquiry through delivery and customer support.

Software engineering activities must be interpreted within the ecosystem in which the software is created and used. Established know-how and infrastructure also create path dependency: once an activity becomes common, the cost of changing it helps the practice persist.
