# Scale

- **0 — Strong reject**: blocks consensus.
- **1 — Reject**: major issues must be resolved now.
- **2 — Weak reject**: minor issues must be resolved now.
- **3 — Weak accept**: minor issues may be resolved later.
- **4 — Accept**: no issues.
- **5 — Strong accept**: willing to lead or champion the proposal.

# Usage

After a statement or proposal, each participant simultaneously shows a fist or up to five fingers. If anyone shows two or fewer fingers, the group can discuss the issue, revise the proposal, and vote again until it reaches its chosen consensus threshold.
