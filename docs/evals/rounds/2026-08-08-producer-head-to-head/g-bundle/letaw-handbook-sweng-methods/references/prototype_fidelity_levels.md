# Levels

- **Low fidelity** — a rough sketch, often hand-drawn or made with a stylus or low-fidelity prototyping tool. Use it to gather feedback on high-level features while large changes are still inexpensive.
- **Medium fidelity** — a careful, detailed illustration, often created with a drawing or presentation tool. Use it to gather feedback on smaller changes to features that are already defined and accepted.
- **High fidelity** — a polished, detailed illustration that resembles the finished interface. Use it to gather feedback on focused, incremental refinements to specific features.

# Paper prototypes

A paper prototype is a hand-drawn interface sketch based on the software's requirements. It is usually low fidelity and can represent interaction with movable paper elements, arrows, annotations, or a sequence of screen drawings.
