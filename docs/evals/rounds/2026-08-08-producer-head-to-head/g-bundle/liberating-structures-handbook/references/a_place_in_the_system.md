A Place in the System is a complexity exercise rather than a Liberating Structure.

# How to run

1. Ask participants to stand in an open area.
2. Each person silently selects one participant as Person A and another as Person B.
3. Instruct everyone to move between their A and B. Let the group mill until it reaches a relatively stable arrangement, then reflect.
4. Change the rule: everyone must become equidistant from A and B. Again allow movement and reflection.

Debrief how the two rules differed and what it felt like to plan while important factors remained outside any one person’s control and everything kept moving.
