The Agreement/Uncertainty Matrix uses two axes:

- the degree of certainty about what is happening; and
- the degree of agreement about what to do or what the situation means.

# Interpretation

- **Simple:** High certainty and agreement; a recipe is the handbook’s analogy.
- **Complicated:** Many parts and formulas, but specialist knowledge can yield a predictable result; building an airplane is the analogy.
- **Complex:** Emergent and unpredictable, so strategies designed for simple or complicated work are insufficient; raising a child is the analogy.

# Use

Introduce the matrix and invite participants to map their current issues or activities. The placement opens discussion about whether their habitual problem-solving strategies fit the situation.
