Air Tic-Tac-Toe is a complexity exercise rather than a Liberating Structure.

# How to run

1. Partners stand facing each other.
2. Without talking or touching, they play several games of three-by-three tic-tac-toe in the air.
3. They then try a four-by-four game under the same constraints.

Most pairs can establish enough shared context for the smaller game but find the larger one impossible. Use the contrast to discuss how meetings often assume people share definitions, assumptions, and mental models that have never been made explicit.
