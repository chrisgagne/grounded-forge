Pair participants so each person takes a turn telling a story and interviewing. Frame the story positively—for example, a time when cross-team collaboration, a customer relationship, or support for an unconventional idea worked especially well.

# Interview practice

Listen with empathy and curiosity. Invite detail with prompts such as:

- Tell me more.
- Why was that important to you?
- What was your contribution?
- What was the organization doing that helped?
- What do you think made it work?
- How has the experience changed you?

The interviewer listens for vivid stories and quotations without replacing the interviewee’s account with their own opinion or story, and allows silence when the speaker needs time.
