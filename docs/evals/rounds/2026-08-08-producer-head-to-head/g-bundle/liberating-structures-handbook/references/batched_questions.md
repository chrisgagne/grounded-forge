Invite three or four participants to ask questions or comment before the speaker responds, rather than alternating one question with one answer.

# Why use it

Batching prevents the first question from steering the whole exchange, lets more voices enter, and gives a better sense of what the group as a whole is asking.

# Facilitation

Questions may be written on cards or sticky notes for later capture. After hearing a batch, the interviewer or facilitator invites the guest to combine questions or respond to the themes they find most useful.
