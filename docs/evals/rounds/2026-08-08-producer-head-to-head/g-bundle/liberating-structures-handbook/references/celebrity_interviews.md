Celebrity Interviews replace a traditional presentation or panel with a talk-show-style exchange between an interviewer and one or more people whose information or ideas can catalyze the group.

# Purpose

The format can convey a few memorable ideas, introduce a key person and their relationship to the topic, establish an interactive tone, and spark interest in more detailed supporting material.

# Facilitation

Coach both interviewer and guests to use questions that open the conversation. Ask what surprised them or what matters most in the evidence rather than requesting a generic summary of the data.
