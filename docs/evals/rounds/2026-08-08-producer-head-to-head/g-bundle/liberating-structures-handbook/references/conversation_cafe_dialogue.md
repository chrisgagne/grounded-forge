# Agreements

- **Open-mindedness:** Listen to and respect every point of view.
- **Acceptance:** Suspend judgment as well as possible.
- **Curiosity:** Seek to understand rather than persuade.
- **Discovery:** Question assumptions and look for new insights.
- **Sincerity:** Speak from the heart and personal experience.
- **Brevity:** Aim for honesty and depth without going on too long.

# Process

Form groups of six to eight and introduce a talking object.

1. In the first round, each person speaks briefly without feedback.
2. In the second round, each person deepens their comments or responds to what now has meaning.
3. Open a spirited dialogue while continuing to use the talking object.
4. In a final round, each person briefly names what challenged, touched, or inspired them.
