Discovery & Action Dialogues are short, lively conversations designed to reveal solutions participants already use, generate additional ways to eliminate or prevent a problem, identify barriers, and recruit volunteers to experiment.

# Question sequence

1. What do you know or think about the issue?
2. What do you do about it?
3. What keeps you from doing that all the time?
4. Who has overcome those barriers, and where?
5. What other ideas do you have for removing barriers or supporting the desired behavior?
6. What has to happen next?
7. Who will do what, and by when?

The process gives facilitators and participants an opportunity to listen directly to one another instead of making assumptions about people most involved in the problem.
