15% Solutions starts from the proposition that most people control only a small share of their work situation while the surrounding structures, systems, events, and culture shape the rest.

# Invitation

Ask:

- Where do you have freedom to act?
- What is in your 15 percent?

The goal is to identify small, significant initiatives that can begin without additional authority and may accumulate into transformational change when guided by a shared vision. The handbook recommends combining this conversation with Troika Consulting.
