# Setup

Create an inner circle of five or six chairs inside a larger outer circle. Very large groups may require several outer rings.

# Process

1. Invite people with direct experience of the challenge into the inner circle.
2. Ask them to talk with one another—not to the audience—as they would in an informal conversation, sharing stories and insights.
3. Invite the outer circle to ask questions and share insights while the inner circle listens. Capture all questions on cards or chart paper.
4. Open dialogue between the inner and outer circles.

# Debrief

Ask what participants heard that surprised them, how their perspective changed, and which questions remain open.
