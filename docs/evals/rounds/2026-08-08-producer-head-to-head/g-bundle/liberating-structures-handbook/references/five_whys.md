The 5 Whys is a question-asking technique for exploring cause-and-effect relationships or peeling back layers of explanation around purpose.

# How to run

1. Pose a generative question and allow at least one minute of individual reflection.
2. Pair participants. Each person takes a turn as questioner and respondent.
3. The questioner listens actively and follows each response with “Why?” or a variation such as “Why is that important?” at least five times.
4. Continue beyond five when needed to reach a fundamental truth.

Pairs may then join another pair to compare what they discovered.
