Have each group or team put what it wants to share on a poster and display all posters together. One team member stays near the poster to answer questions while teammates rotate through that role and visit the rest of the gallery.

# Debrief questions

- What did we notice?
- What themes emerged?
- What was surprising?
- What was new?
- How does this change our conversation?

A long wall helps participants perceive the full range of contributions at once.
