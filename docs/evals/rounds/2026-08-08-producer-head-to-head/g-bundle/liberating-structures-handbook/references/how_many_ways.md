How Many Ways? is a complexity exercise rather than a Liberating Structure.

# How to run

1. Prepare bags containing 12–25 varied small objects. Bags should be similar but not identical.
2. Give one bag to each group and ask them to organize the objects by criteria such as color, function, or material without labeling the groupings.
3. Invite groups to try several organizations and choose the most “elegant,” easiest to guess, hardest to guess, or governed by one meta-structure.
4. Have groups inspect one another’s arrangements and infer the organizing criteria.

# Debrief

Explore whether there is one right answer, what assumptions shaped each arrangement, how groups negotiated, and what was surprising in other solutions.
