# How to run

1. Ask everyone to stand, leave their belongings behind, and spread into open space.
2. Offer a provocative, accessible question related to the meeting purpose and allow individual thought.
3. At a signal, each person finds someone they know less well and discusses the question for five to ten minutes.
4. Signal a change of partner and repeat, usually for three rounds. Participants seeking a partner can raise a hand.
5. Use a continuous signal to end the final round.

The structure generates energy and ensures everyone speaks early. A first contribution lowers the barrier to later participation and distributes airtime regardless of total group size.
