# One Word at a Time Story

Players sit in a circle and create a story, each contributing one word in turn. A player may say “period” to mark a sentence ending.

# Yes And

Each response must begin “Yes, and…” and build on the previous player’s contribution. The constraint makes it easy to notice when someone rejects an offer, takes control of the scene, or answers with “yes, but.”

# Coffee Filters

Give everyone a coffee filter to toss, float, balance, and catch. Participants experiment with partners, invent moves, imitate one another, and notice what happens.

These games help participants experience the difference between opening and closing a conversation.
