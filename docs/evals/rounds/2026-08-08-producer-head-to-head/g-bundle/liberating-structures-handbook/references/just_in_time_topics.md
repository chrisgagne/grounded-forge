# How to run

1. If possible, begin in a circle and provide drawing paper and colored markers.
2. Invite each participant to write a question, story, or problem they would like to explore with others, often for 45–90 minutes.
3. Have people declare their topics briefly, without explaining them in detail, then post them where everyone can see.
4. Assign each topic a location and invite participants to choose where to spend their time.

Avoid combining topics merely because they are related; smaller discussions are often more satisfying. The process borrows Open Space principles: whoever comes are the right people, and anyone not contributing or learning may use the “law of two feet” to move elsewhere.
