Knee-to-Knee Conversation brings people physically closer in small groups without a table between them, supporting intimacy and direct exchange.

# How to run

Arrange chairs in small knee-to-knee groups before people arrive. Invite participants to sit with people they know less well and discuss a question connected to the gathering’s purpose.

# Example invitations

- What drew you to this meeting?
- What are you most excited about in your work right now?
- What are you bringing to this community?
- What audacious goal could we have?
