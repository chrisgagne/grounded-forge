The Levels of Accountability classification moves from disempowered, defensive responses toward empowered learning and growth.

# Levels

1. **Denial:** “This has nothing to do with me.”
2. **Blame others:** “It’s their fault.”
3. **Blame self:** “It’s my fault,” expressed as guilt.
4. **Wait and hope:** “Somebody should do something.”
5. **Acknowledge the situation:** “I’m a piece of the puzzle.”
6. **Accept ownership:** “It starts with me.”
7. **Find solutions:** “This can work.”
8. **Take action and learn:** Make it happen and renew.

# Invitation

Ask what the group needs to do to work “above the line,” moving from protection and defense toward ownership, solutions, and learning.
