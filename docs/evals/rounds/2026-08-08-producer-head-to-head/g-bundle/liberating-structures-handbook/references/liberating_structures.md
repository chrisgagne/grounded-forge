Liberating Structures are processes or rules that provide just enough structure for people to create, innovate, and produce results together without being constrained or made powerless.

# Defining qualities

- Participation crosses boundaries between roles, departments, and parts of a system that do not ordinarily interact.
- Order can emerge from local, self-organized interaction.
- The dialogue is generative rather than merely informational.
- Simple constraints channel energy while preserving many degrees of freedom.
- People participate in developing ideas, decisions, and actions, creating ownership rather than asking others for buy-in after the fact.

# Design primitives

Each structure combines choices about the rhythm and timing of interaction, group size and inclusion boundaries, the physical setting, and the conceptual container created by the invitation or question.
