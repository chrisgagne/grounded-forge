# How to run

1. Place an object on the floor to represent an idea, choice, or proposed move.
2. Ask participants to position themselves according to how positively they feel: close for strong enthusiasm, farther away for less.
3. Beginning with the people farthest away, invite participants to speak from the perspective represented by their distance, moving inward ring by ring.
4. Run another round with a different question, such as how likely the proposal is to succeed.

Comparing felt enthusiasm with assessed likelihood can expose the source of differences in a group.
