Invite participants to spend about 20 minutes mapping the people and organizations that matter to a project, goal, or initiative, then compare maps in groups of three or four.

# Analysis prompts

- What is surprising or unexpected?
- Who or what type of organization is missing?
- Who are the hubs with many links, and who are the isolates?

# Weaving moves

- **Close triangles:** Introduce two people who do not know one another but would benefit from a connection.
- **Integrate clusters:** Look for projects or interests that could connect otherwise separate clusters.
- **Expand the periphery:** Add underrepresented people, organizations, neighborhoods, identities, resources, skills, ideas, or perspectives.
