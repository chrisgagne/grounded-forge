Give participants a full, timed minute of silence to reflect on a question before beginning an exercise or discussion.

# Purpose

The pause lets everyone become present and form an initial response before faster speakers shape the conversation. It benefits the whole group, not only participants who prefer quiet reflection.

# Facilitation

State that the minute will be timed exactly so people know what to expect. During a later debrief, invite participants to notice what the silence contributed.
