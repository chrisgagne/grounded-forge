# Sequence

1. **Individual:** Give everyone a few silent minutes to reflect on a question and optionally make notes.
2. **Twos:** Invite each person to share ideas with one partner for roughly five to ten minutes.
3. **Small groups:** Join pairs into groups of four to six. Members first share what was interesting in earlier rounds, then continue the discussion.
4. **Whole group:** Reassemble and ask what insights emerged or how participants’ understanding changed.

Groups larger than six reduce each person’s opportunity to speak. The structure can follow Impromptu Speed Networking.
