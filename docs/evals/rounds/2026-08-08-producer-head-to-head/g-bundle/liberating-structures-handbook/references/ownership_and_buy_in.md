# Ownership

Ownership means owning or sharing ownership of an idea, decision, action plan, or choice because one participated in its development and made the choice freely.

# Buy-in

Buy-in occurs when one person or group develops and decides an idea, then tries to convince others to implement it without involving them in the originating conversations or decisions.

# Practical distinction

The handbook treats calls for buy-in as a warning that the development and implementation process may be missing people who should be involved. For difficult socio-technical behavior change, people in the system need to own the new behaviors.
