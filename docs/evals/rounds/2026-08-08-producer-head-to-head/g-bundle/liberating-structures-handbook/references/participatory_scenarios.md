Participatory Scenarios broadens scenario development beyond experts or a small planning team so the wider community develops both the scenarios and ownership of them.

# How to run

1. Identify five or six important drivers that can each be described along a continuum.
2. Select two drivers and use them as the axes of a matrix.
3. Populate the resulting landscape with characteristics, consequences, questions, opportunities, and challenges.
4. Name each scenario and write a story that makes it vivid.
5. Repeat with other driver combinations.

The process also helps participants define indicators that suggest one scenario or another is emerging.
