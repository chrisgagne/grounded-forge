Portfolio Planning helps a group see the whole collection of projects or activities rather than discussing each in isolation.

# How to run

Introduce the ecocycle using forest-fire phases as an analogy, emphasizing that creative destruction opens space for renewal. Invite small groups to place activities in the different parts of the cycle, or use a wall-sized ecocycle and movable sticky notes so participants can position and reposition the portfolio together.
