# Preparation

Recruit teams with experience stories, research results, or project plans to share. Coach them to prepare show-and-tell material at an executive-briefing level, leaving time for questions and interaction. Assign each team a location.

# Rotation

Participants travel among team locations in timed rounds. Match the number and length of rotations to the available time and number of hosts. For example, in one hour, participants could visit four locations for 15 minutes each, or choose three of five locations for 20 minutes each.

The distributed setup avoids a long sequence of didactic presentations.
