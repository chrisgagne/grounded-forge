Invite participants to distill their thinking about a complex or vague topic into a fixed, very small number of words.

# Mission-statement constraints

The handbook cites a variant using fewer than eight words that includes:

- a verb;
- a target population; and
- a measurable outcome.

# Useful prompts

Ask what gets participants up in the morning, what they do and why, whom they can help, or what influence they can have. The playful constraint can reveal the core of a mission, vision, purpose, or strategy.
