Give participants a substantial block of time—often ten minutes, but adjustable—to think independently about a generative question and optionally make private notes.

# Example questions

- How has your thinking changed since the session began?
- What have you heard that is energizing your thinking?
- What question do you still need to answer to get unstuck?
- What has been missing from the conversation?

# Sequence

Follow the individual reflection with a structure that lets participants share in a small group.
