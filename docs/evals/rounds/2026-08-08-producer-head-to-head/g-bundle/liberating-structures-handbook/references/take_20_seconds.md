After asking a group a substantive question, wait at least 20 seconds before speaking again.

# Why it works

Participants may be forming answers and gathering the courage to respond. Speaking too soon truncates that process and signals that the facilitator is the person with the answers.

# Facilitation

Practice counting 20 seconds internally or ask a partner to time the pause. Looking down at your shoes breaks eye contact, signals reflection time, and reduces the pressure on any one participant to answer immediately.
