# How to run

1. Place one sheet of flip-chart paper on the wall for each participant.
2. Offer roughly eight to ten generative questions related to the meeting purpose.
3. Give participants about 20 minutes to create a stand-alone response on their panel using words, drawings, diagrams, or mind maps.
4. Form local groups of three or four. Each person brings their panel to life for about four minutes while others ask only clarifying questions.

Take a Panel can serve as a soft start, provided every participant receives enough working time, and should be followed by another structure for discussing what was learned.
