TRIZ originated as the Theory of Inventive Problem Solving. This structure uses one part of the broader approach to examine a difficult, complex problem.

# How to run

1. Describe the desired result and its key elements as specifically as possible.
2. Design a comprehensive system that makes it absolutely impossible to achieve that result. Identify policies, practices, and operating habits that guarantee failure.
3. Ask which parts of this adverse system resemble the current state.
4. Identify what it would take to eliminate those similarities.

The inversion makes familiar but harmful behavior easier to recognize and discuss.
