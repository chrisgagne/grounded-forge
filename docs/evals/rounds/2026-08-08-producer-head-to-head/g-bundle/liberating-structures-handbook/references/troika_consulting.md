# How to run

1. Form knee-to-knee groups of three and designate a timekeeper.
2. Give everyone individual reflection time to identify a difficult question, obstacle, or application challenge; note-taking can help.
3. In a ten-minute round, one participant shares their challenge and possible next steps.
4. The two partners first ask questions that sharpen the participant’s thinking, then talk with each other about possible approaches, and finally bring the participant back into the exchange.
5. Switch roles until everyone has received a consultation.
6. Discuss insights and patterns across the three rounds.

Equal timing ensures each person receives a full turn.
