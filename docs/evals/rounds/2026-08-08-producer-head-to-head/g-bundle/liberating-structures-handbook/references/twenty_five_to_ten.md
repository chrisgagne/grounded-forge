# How to run

1. Give everyone a card, pose a question, and ask each person to write their best idea clearly without adding a name.
2. Participants mill and repeatedly exchange cards until signaled to stop.
3. In pairs, each person reads the card they hold persuasively. They trade cards, rate each from 1 to 5, discuss and adjust the rating, then record it on the back.
4. Repeat the mill-and-rate cycle for five rounds. Each card should receive five ratings and therefore a total score from 5 to 25.
5. Count down from 25. As each score is reached, people holding a card with that total read it aloud until the top ten or desired number of ideas has surfaced.

Keep every card; a lower-rated idea may become useful later. The method senses crowd judgment but is not recommended as a decision rule.
