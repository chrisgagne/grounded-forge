Use the questions in order at the end of a meeting or at a useful midpoint.

# What?

Ask what is emerging, which data stand out, and what actually happened.

# So what?

Ask what the data imply, what patterns are visible, and whether they make a difference.

# Now what?

Ask what action could help the group move forward and who else should be involved.

Keeping the sequence separates shared observation from meaning-making before the group commits to action.
