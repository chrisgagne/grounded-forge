A Wicked Question contains a genuine paradox or tension and has no obvious answer. It is not a trick question whose answer someone already knows.

# Purpose

Articulating both sides of a tension surfaces hidden assumptions and differences. Those patterns can help a group discover common ground or alternatives for stubborn problems.

# Examples

- How can we sustain quality standards across the system while allowing local innovation?
- How can we maintain the top-down discipline needed for safety while leveling the playing field for bottom-up creativity?

A complaint disguised as a question does not qualify because it does not hold a productive tension.
