# Group

Form knee-to-knee groups of five or six. One person is the client and the others are the consulting team.

# How to run

1. For about ten minutes, the client sketches a complex challenge and the questions they are struggling with. Consultants ask only a few clarifying questions.
2. The client turns away and listens silently while the consulting team talks with one another for at least 15 minutes. Consultants may offer questions, paraphrases, metaphors, examples, and possibilities, but should not prescribe what the client must do.
3. The client turns back and begins with what intrigued, surprised, or resonated with them. The group then continues in open conversation.
