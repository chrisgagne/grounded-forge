World Café engages a large group in conversations that feel like parts of one connected whole.

# Setting

Create a café-like environment with small tables, four chairs per table, paper, colored pens, and optional talking objects.

# Process

1. Welcome participants, establish context, explain the process and café etiquette, and put people at ease.
2. Run three or more small-group conversation rounds of about 20 minutes.
3. After each round, participants move to new tables. One person may remain as table host to welcome the next group and briefly connect it to the previous conversation.
4. Use context-specific questions, either repeating a question or building successive questions.
5. Between or after rounds, invite participants to harvest insights for the whole group, often with visual recording.
