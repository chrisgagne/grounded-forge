Combined arms is the full integration of arms so that an enemy who counters one arm becomes more vulnerable to another. The purpose is to impose a dilemma rather than merely a problem.

Combined arms is achieved through tactics and techniques at lower levels and task organization at higher levels. Each arm performs the missions for which it is best suited, while complementary capabilities increase mobility, firepower, and overall combat power.

At fire-team level, an automatic weapon can pin an enemy in place and expose that enemy to a grenade launcher; movement to escape grenades then creates vulnerability to automatic fire. At MAGTF level, assault support aircraft can concentrate ground forces, artillery and close air support can support a penetration, and deep air support can interdict reinforcement. Each enemy response to one capability creates exposure to another.
