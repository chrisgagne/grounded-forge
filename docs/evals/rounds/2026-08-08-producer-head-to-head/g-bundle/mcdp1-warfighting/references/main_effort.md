The main effort is the unit assigned responsibility for the action that is most critical to success at a given moment. It is the focal point on which the force's combat power converges.

The main effort:

- Receives priority for support.
- Establishes for every other unit the requirement to support its mission.
- Concentrates decisive combat power and accepts risk through economy elsewhere.
- Is directed against an object whose defeat will have the greatest effect on the enemy and offers a strong opportunity for success.

Each commander should establish a main effort for each operation. As the situation changes, the commander may shift it and redirect combat power to the unit that has become most critical. In general, a shift should exploit success instead of reinforcing failure.
