A Marine air-ground task force (MAGTF) is a task organization consisting of:

- A command element.
- A ground combat element.
- An aviation combat element.
- A combat service support element.

A MAGTF has no single standard structure. It is constituted and, when necessary, restructured for the specific situation. The organization gives one commander a combined-arms force that can be tailored to the mission and operating environment.

Marine forces form MAGTFs for operations and training. Training with the full complement of assigned, reinforcing, and supporting forces builds the combined-arms proficiency required for combat.
