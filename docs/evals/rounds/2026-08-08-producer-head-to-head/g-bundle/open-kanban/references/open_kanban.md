# Definition

Open Kanban is an Agile and Lean ultra-light method for improving work in any part of an organization, with a primary focus on IT and software development. It is deliberately a method core rather than a complete Agile or Lean method: its defining components are shared values, practices that put those values into action, and an open license that permits extension and modification.

# Values

Open Kanban defines five values:

1. **Respect for people** — empower people to pull work and take responsibility while maintaining a sustainable pace.
2. **Courage** — enable people to identify mistakes, challenge authority constructively, and support continuous improvement.
3. **Focus on value** — create outcomes that satisfy customers and eliminate work that does not add value.
4. **Communication and collaboration** — make teamwork possible by combining clear communication with cooperative value creation.
5. **Holistic or systemic approach to change** — improve the whole system rather than optimizing isolated parts, recognizing people as central to how the system works.

# Practices

Open Kanban translates its values into four practices:

1. **Visualize the workflow.** Make otherwise invisible knowledge work and its movement through stages visible with Kanban boards, diagrams, dashboards, performance metrics, or other information radiators.
2. **Lead using a team approach.** Deliver value through functioning teams and team leadership without requiring new organizational roles or titles.
3. **Reduce the batch size of your efforts.** Reduce the complexity and quantity of work handled at each stage so the team can focus, improve flow, and finish sooner. Open Kanban treats limiting work in progress as a consequence of reducing batch size rather than as a required practice in its own right.
4. **Learn and improve continuously.** Build learning into the culture and workflow, then apply it through mechanisms such as retrospectives, strategy meetings, or Kaizen groups.

# Open Kanban methods

An Open Kanban method extends and customizes the common Open Kanban core for a particular audience or context. Such methods may be free or commercial, but share the Agile and Lean core and are encouraged to contribute improvements back to the main Open Kanban repository.
