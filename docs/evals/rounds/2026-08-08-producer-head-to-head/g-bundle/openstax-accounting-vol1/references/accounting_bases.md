# Recognition bases

| Basis | Revenue recognition | Expense recognition |
|---|---|---|
| Cash basis | When cash is received. | When cash is paid. |
| Accrual basis | When revenue is earned, even if cash has not yet been received. | When the expense is incurred, even if it has not yet been paid. |

Cash-basis accounting waits for an exchange of cash before recording a transaction in the financial statements. Accrual-basis accounting records the economic event in the period it occurs and therefore separates recognition from collection or payment.

# Period consequences

Under accrual accounting, a credit sale can produce revenue and an account receivable before collection, while an incurred but unpaid cost can produce an expense and a liability before payment. Adjusting entries align such revenues and expenses with the appropriate accounting period.
