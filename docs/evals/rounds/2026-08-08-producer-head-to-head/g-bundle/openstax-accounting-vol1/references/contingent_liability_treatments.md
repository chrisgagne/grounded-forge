A contingency is a current situation whose outcome remains uncertain until a future event. A contingent liability is a contingency that could create a future debt or other negative obligation, such as pending litigation, a warranty claim, an insurance claim, or bankruptcy.

# Recognition inputs

Treatment depends on:

1. the likelihood that the obligation will occur; and
2. whether the amount of loss can be reasonably estimated.

A reasonable estimate may be grounded in past experience, industry standards, or a known potential financial outcome.

# Treatment matrix

| Classification | Meaning | Journalize and recognize | Note disclosure |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Probable and estimable | Likely to occur and reasonably measurable. | Yes | Yes |
| Probable and inestimable | Likely to occur but not reasonably measurable. | No | Yes |
| Reasonably possible | Could occur but is not probable; the amount may or may not be estimable. | No | Yes |
| Remote | Unlikely to occur; the amount may or may not be estimable. | No | No |

When a probable but initially inestimable loss later becomes reasonably estimable, financial recognition becomes appropriate.
