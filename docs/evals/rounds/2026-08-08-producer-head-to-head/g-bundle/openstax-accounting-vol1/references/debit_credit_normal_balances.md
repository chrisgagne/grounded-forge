# Entry sides

- A **debit** records an amount on the left side of an account and is commonly abbreviated `DR` or `dr`.
- A **credit** records an amount on the right side of an account and is commonly abbreviated `CR` or `cr`.
- An account's ending balance is the difference between its debits and credits.

Double-entry bookkeeping changes at least two accounts for every transaction, includes at least one debit and one credit, and requires:

```text
Total debits = Total credits
```

# Normal balances

The normal balance is the side on which an account class increases.

| Account class | Increases with | Decreases with | Normal balance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asset | Debit | Credit | Debit |
| Liability | Credit | Debit | Credit |
| Common stock or other contributed equity | Credit | Debit | Credit |
| Dividend or owner distribution | Debit | Credit | Debit |
| Revenue | Credit | Debit | Credit |
| Expense | Debit | Credit | Debit |

A balance on the side opposite an account's expected normal balance is an abnormal balance. A contra account intentionally uses a normal balance opposite that of the broader account category it offsets.
