# Core elements

| Element | Meaning | Statement role |
|---|---|---|
| Asset | A tangible or intangible resource owned or controlled by an entity that is expected to provide economic value. | Reported on the balance sheet. |
| Liability | A present obligation that is expected to require a future transfer of assets or provision of services. | Reported on the balance sheet. |
| Equity | The residual interest in an entity's assets after deducting its liabilities. | Reported on the balance sheet and explained by an equity statement. |
| Revenue | Value generated by delivering goods, rendering services, or conducting other central operations. | Increases the period's income and equity. |
| Expense | A cost associated with providing goods or services. | Decreases the period's income and equity. |
| Gain | An increase in organizational value from an activity incidental or peripheral to the entity's main purpose. | Contributes to net income but is distinguished from revenue. |
| Loss | A decrease in organizational value from an activity incidental or peripheral to the entity's main purpose. | Contributes to net loss or reduces net income but is distinguished from expense. |

# Accounting equation

The balance-sheet elements are related by:

```text
Assets = Liabilities + Equity
```

Assets are the organization's resources; liabilities and equity represent creditor and owner claims against those resources.

# Asset classifications

- **Current or short-term asset** — expected to be used, sold, or converted to cash within one year or the normal operating cycle. Examples include cash, accounts receivable, and inventory.
- **Noncurrent or long-term asset** — used in normal operations for more than one year and not intended for resale. Examples include land, buildings, equipment, and vehicles.
- **Tangible asset** — has physical substance.
- **Intangible asset** — has economic value without physical substance, such as a copyright, patent, goodwill, or trademark.

# Liability classifications

- **Current or short-term liability** — expected to be settled within one year or, when longer, the entity's normal operating cycle. Examples include accounts payable and short-term notes payable.
- **Noncurrent or long-term liability** — expected to be settled beyond that current period. Examples include long-term notes, mortgages, and bonds payable.
