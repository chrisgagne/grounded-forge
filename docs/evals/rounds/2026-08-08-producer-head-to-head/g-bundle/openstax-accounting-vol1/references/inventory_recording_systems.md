# System types

| Characteristic | Periodic system | Perpetual system |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory update | At the end of the reporting period. | Continuously as purchases and sales occur. |
| Purchase during the period | Recorded in a Purchases account. | Recorded directly in Merchandise Inventory. |
| Sale during the period | Records the sales entry; inventory and cost of goods sold wait for the period-end adjustment. | Records the sales entry and a second entry that transfers the item's cost from inventory to cost of goods sold. |
| Period-end work | Physical count determines ending inventory, and an adjustment brings the records to that amount. | A physical count can identify shrinkage, but routine purchases and sales are already reflected in the account. |

# Definitions

- A **periodic inventory system** updates inventory at the end of a month, quarter, year, or other reporting period to agree with the physical count.
- A **perpetual inventory system** updates the inventory balance for each purchase and sale, often using barcode, radio-frequency identification, or other automatic-identification technology.

The choice of recording system is separate from the inventory cost-flow method used to allocate costs between cost of goods sold and ending inventory.
