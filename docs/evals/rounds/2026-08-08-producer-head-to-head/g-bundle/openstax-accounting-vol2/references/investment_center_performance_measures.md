# Return on Investment

Return on investment (ROI) expresses income generated relative to the assets or invested capital used:

```text
ROI = Operating income / Average invested capital
```

The DuPont form separates profitability and asset use:

```text
Sales margin = Operating income / Sales

Asset turnover = Sales / Average invested capital

ROI = Sales margin × Asset turnover
```

ROI permits percentage comparisons across differently sized segments, but a manager evaluated on current ROI may reject a project that exceeds the company’s required return but would reduce the segment’s existing ROI.

# Residual Income

Residual income (RI) measures income earned above the organization’s minimum required return:

```text
RI
  = Operating income
  − (Minimum required rate of return × Average invested capital)
```

A positive RI indicates that the segment or project earns more than the minimum. RI supports goal congruence better than ROI in some investment decisions, but its currency amount is not directly comparable across differently sized segments.

# Economic Value Added

Economic value added (EVA) measures after-tax income remaining after the cost of invested capital:

```text
EVA
  = After-tax operating income
  − (Weighted average cost of capital × Invested capital)
```

```text
WACC
  = Σ(Capital-source weight × After-tax source cost)
```

A positive EVA indicates value created after covering the weighted cost of debt and equity. EVA can differ from RI because it uses after-tax income and the organization’s weighted cost of capital rather than a management-selected minimum return.

# Measurement Integrity

Comparisons must use consistent definitions of income and assets. Historical cost versus depreciated asset values, beginning versus average balances, taxes, and controllability can materially change reported performance without a corresponding change in operating decisions.
