# Relevance Rule

For a short-term decision, information is relevant when it concerns the future and differs between alternatives. Differential analysis compares only the relevant costs and revenues of the available alternatives.

# Cost and Revenue Classifications

- **Differential cost or revenue:** the amount by which a cost or revenue differs between alternatives; it is relevant.
- **Avoidable cost:** a future cost eliminated in whole or in part by selecting an alternative; it is relevant.
- **Unavoidable cost:** a cost that remains under the alternatives being considered; it is irrelevant to their difference.
- **Sunk cost:** a cost already incurred; it cannot be changed by the current decision and is irrelevant.
- **Opportunity cost:** the benefit forgone by rejecting the next-best alternative; it is relevant even though it is not recorded in the accounting system.
- **Allocated cost:** a shared cost assigned by a formula. It is relevant only to the extent the organization’s total future cost changes.
- **Joint cost:** a cost incurred before the split-off point. For a sell-or-process-further decision, the joint cost is sunk with respect to further processing; compare incremental revenue with incremental processing cost.
- **Qualitative factor:** a nonnumeric consideration such as quality, employee morale, supplier reliability, customer relationships, safety, or brand effects.

# Common Decisions

- **Special order:** compare incremental order revenue with incremental costs and any opportunity cost from displaced regular sales.
- **Make or buy:** compare avoidable internal costs and opportunity costs with the supplier’s price; exclude unavoidable fixed allocations.
- **Keep or discontinue:** compare lost contribution margin with costs actually avoided; allocated common costs that remain do not support discontinuance.
- **Sell or process further:** process further when incremental revenue exceeds incremental processing cost.
- **Constrained resource:** rank products by unit contribution margin per unit of the constrained resource, subject to demand and other constraints.

```text
Contribution margin per constrained resource
  = Unit contribution margin / Units of constrained resource required
```
