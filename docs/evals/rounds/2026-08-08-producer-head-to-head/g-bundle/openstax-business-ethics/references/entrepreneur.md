# Entrepreneur

An **entrepreneur** is a business leader willing to accept the risk of starting a company and offering a product or service in the hope that the firm will be sustainable and profitable.

Entrepreneurs commonly supply the founding vision, seek the resources needed for the venture, and shape the initial practices of the business. Start-ups carry a substantial risk of financial failure, while rapid success can introduce new risks as informal teams develop additional management layers and formal procedures.

## Entrepreneurial culture

**Entrepreneurial culture** is the combination of a founder's personality and management style that shapes a start-up's initial business practices and ethical environment. If the enterprise succeeds, the founder's principles can remain embedded in the company after the founder leaves.

Because early behavior sets expectations, founders' treatment of employees, customers, and other stakeholders can establish either an ethical culture or its absence from the first days of the firm. Growth tests whether commitments such as honesty, courtesy, respect, and responsible customer service can be transmitted to later employees and leaders.
