# Stakeholder

A **stakeholder** is an individual or entity affected by a business's decisions. Stakeholders may include customers, clients, employees, shareholders, suppliers, communities, the natural environment, government, and media organizations. One person or group can occupy more than one stakeholder role at the same time.

## Relationships to the business

- **Internal stakeholders** include the board of directors, executives, and employees who define, implement, or work within the organization's mission and operations.
- **External stakeholders** include customers, suppliers, shareholders, communities, regulators, the environment, and other parties that influence or are affected by the business.

Every stakeholder may have a **stakeholder claim**: a particular interest in a business decision. Claims can conflict, and legal compliance captures only part of the ethical obligations a firm may owe its stakeholders.

## Stakeholder categories

- **Enabling stakeholders** permit the firm to function within its economic and legal system, such as stockholders, legislatures, government regulators, and boards of directors.
- **Normative stakeholders** influence industry norms or informal rules, such as competitors and peers.
- **Functional stakeholders** influence inputs or outputs, such as suppliers, employees, unions, customers, distributors, and retailers.
- **Diffused stakeholders** have less direct relationships but may still have significant effects, such as nongovernmental organizations, voters, and mass media.

## Approaches to stakeholder claims

- The **descriptive approach** models the company as groups with distinct interests and seeks to weigh those interests fairly.
- The **instrumental approach** connects sound stakeholder management to financial outcomes.
- The **normative approach** treats stakeholders as ends in themselves whose interests have intrinsic value, not merely as means to improve the bottom line.

**Stakeholder prioritization** is the process of deciding which stakeholders to address and in what sequence when their expectations conflict or resources are limited.
