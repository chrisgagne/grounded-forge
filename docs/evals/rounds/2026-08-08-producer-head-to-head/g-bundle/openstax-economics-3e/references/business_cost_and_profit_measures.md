# Revenue

For price $P$ and quantity sold $Q$:

$$
Total\ revenue = P \times Q
$$

# Cost measures

- **Fixed cost (FC):** expenditure on fixed inputs that does not change with output in the short run.
- **Variable cost (VC):** production cost that changes with the quantity produced.
- **Total cost (TC):** $FC + VC$.
- **Average total cost (ATC):** $TC/Q$.
- **Average variable cost (AVC):** $VC/Q$.
- **Marginal cost (MC):** the additional cost of producing one more unit, measured as $\Delta TC/\Delta Q$.

The short run contains at least one fixed input. In the long run all inputs are variable, so a firm can compare alternative production technologies and choose the lowest-cost way to produce each output level.

# Profit measures

- **Accounting profit:** total revenue minus explicit, out-of-pocket costs, including depreciation.
- **Economic profit:** total revenue minus both explicit costs and implicit opportunity costs of resources the firm already owns.
- **Average profit (profit margin):** profit divided by quantity produced.

Because economic profit includes implicit costs, it cannot exceed accounting profit for the same revenue and explicit costs.

# Scale interpretation

- **Economies of scale:** long-run average cost falls as output rises.
- **Constant returns to scale:** proportional expansion of all inputs leaves average production cost unchanged.
- **Diseconomies of scale:** long-run average cost rises as output rises.
