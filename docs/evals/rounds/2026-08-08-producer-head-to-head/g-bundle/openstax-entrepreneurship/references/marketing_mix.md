# Definition

The marketing mix is the set of coordinated strategies a venture uses to identify and reach its target market. Promotion is only one element of the mix.

# The 7Ps

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Product | The good or service that creates value by fulfilling a customer's need or desire. Products may be tangible goods, intangible services, or a bundle of both. |
| Price | The value a customer exchanges to receive the product or service, usually money. Pricing directly affects demand, sales, competitiveness, and profit potential. |
| Promotion | Communication of the product's value in a way that encourages purchase. Methods include advertising, social media, public relations, direct mail, sales promotions, and personal selling. |
| Place | The physical or digital channels through which customers buy the product, also called distribution. Direct channels sell without an intermediary; indirect channels use parties such as agents, distributors, wholesalers, or retailers. |
| People | The employees and other human resources who deliver the offering and represent the brand, especially in service businesses where customer interaction is part of the product. |
| Physical environment | The tangible setting and cues surrounding a service, including décor, cleanliness, smell, music, temperature, and color, that shape expectations about quality and professionalism. |
| Process | The chain of procedures and activities through which the service is provided, from the customer's first step through completion and payment. |

# Product and service emphasis

The first four elements—product, price, promotion, and place—are the traditional 4Ps and apply especially to goods. People, physical environment, and process extend the mix for services. All seven can matter to any venture, and they should work together to create value for both the business and its customers.
