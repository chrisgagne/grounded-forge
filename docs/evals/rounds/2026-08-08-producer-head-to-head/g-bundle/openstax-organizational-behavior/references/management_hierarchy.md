# Management

**Management** is the process of planning, organizing, directing, and controlling employee activities together with other resources to accomplish organizational objectives. A manager coordinates and oversees the work of others toward ends that individuals could not attain alone.

# Levels

| Level | Organizational scope | Typical emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Executive management** | The entire organization | Strategic direction, long-range planning, business indicators, coordination, and organization-wide decisions |
| **Middle management** | Major departments or divisions, often including lower-level managers | Translating strategy into departmental plans and coordinating across units |
| **First-line management** | Nonmanagerial employees and day-to-day departmental work | Supervision, task execution, scheduling, and technical operations |

# Managerial skills

- **Technical skills:** using the tools, procedures, and techniques of a specialty.
- **Human-relations skills:** working with people and understanding motivation and group processes.
- **Conceptual skills:** organizing and analyzing information, seeing the organization as a whole, and understanding how its parts fit together.

Executive work generally demands more conceptual skill, while first-line work generally demands more technical skill. Human-relations skills remain important at every level.
