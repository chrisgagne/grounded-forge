# Definition

**Organizational change** is movement by an organization from its current state toward a future state. **Change management** is the process of designing and implementing such change.

# Change content

- **Structural change:** alters formal relationships, departments, positions, roles, assignments, centralization, or other features of organizational architecture.
- **Technological change:** introduces technology that changes tools, procedures, work, collaboration, or relationships; it often induces structural change.
- **Culture change:** reshapes shared assumptions, values, mindsets, behavior, or the organization’s core identity.

# Dimensions

## Scope

- **Incremental change:** limited refinements that build on current practices or routines.
- **Transformational change:** significant shifts that disrupt an underlying aspect of the organization, its processes, or its structures.
- **Strategic change:** incremental or transformational change that aligns operations with the organization’s strategic mission and objectives.

## Level

- **Individual-level change:** develops an employee’s performance, knowledge, or capability.
- **Group-level change:** improves relationships and effectiveness within a team or other group.
- **Organization-level change:** affects the whole organizational system or several of its units.

## Intentionality

- **Planned change:** deliberately designed activities intended to move toward a specified goal.
- **Unplanned change:** change that emerges without deliberate design, often through informal organizing or as an unexpected consequence of planned action.

Higher-level change commonly requires supporting changes at lower levels, and unplanned effects can emerge during an otherwise planned process.
