# Definition

**Entrepreneurs** are people with vision, drive, and creativity who are willing to take the risk of starting and managing a business for profit or of greatly changing the scope and direction of an existing firm. Entrepreneurship typically centers on innovation, spotting trends, and pursuing a new product, service, or business model.

# Entrepreneur and Small-Business Owner

An entrepreneur may own a small business, but the terms are not interchangeable. Entrepreneurship involves risk taken to create a business or substantially change an existing one. A small-business owner may instead start or buy a business and deliberately keep it small, focusing on management or technical practice without seeking a disruptive model or substantial growth.

# Common Types

- **Classic entrepreneur:** starts a company based on an innovative idea.
- **Micropreneur:** starts small and intends to remain small, often for personal satisfaction or a preferred lifestyle.
- **Growth-oriented entrepreneur:** intends to develop the venture into a major enterprise.
- **Multipreneur:** starts a series of companies and repeatedly takes on the challenge of building ventures.
- **Intrapreneur:** applies creativity, vision, and risk-taking inside a larger corporation while the employer supplies salary and financial backing.
