# Management Levels

- **Executive managers** occupy the top of the hierarchy and are responsible for the organization as a whole, especially its strategic direction.
- **Middle managers** are responsible for major departments and may supervise lower-level managers.
- **First-line managers** directly supervise nonmanagerial employees and oversee day-to-day departmental work.

# Managerial Skills

- **Technical skills:** the ability to use the tools, procedures, and techniques of a specialized area.
- **Human relations skills:** the ability to work with people and understand employee motivation and group processes.
- **Conceptual skills:** the ability to organize and analyze information, see the organization as a whole, and understand how its parts work together.

# Relative Emphasis

Executive work generally places greater emphasis on conceptual skills and long-range planning, while first-line management places greater emphasis on technical skills and supervision. Human relations skills remain important at every level.
