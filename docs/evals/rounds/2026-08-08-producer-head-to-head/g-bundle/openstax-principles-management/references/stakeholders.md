# Definition

A **stakeholder** is any group or individual who can affect or is affected by an organization's strategies, major transactions, activities, or achievement of objectives. Typical stakeholders include employees, suppliers, customers, shareholders, government, media, and communities.

# Stakeholder Management

**Stakeholder management** is the systematic identification, analysis, planning, and implementation of actions designed to engage stakeholders. It complements corporate social responsibility by asking organizations to consider relationships that affect or are affected by organizational purposes and to use transparency, fairness, and stakeholder interests in strategic decisions.

# Analysis Questions

For a proposed action, issue, or change, identify:

1. The stakeholders who may support or resist it.
2. Each stakeholder's interest or stake.
3. What supporters stand to gain or lose.
4. What resisters stand to gain or lose.
5. The kinds of power supporters can exercise.
6. The kinds of power resisters can exercise.
7. Strategies for retaining support.
8. Strategies for neutralizing resistance or winning resisters over.
