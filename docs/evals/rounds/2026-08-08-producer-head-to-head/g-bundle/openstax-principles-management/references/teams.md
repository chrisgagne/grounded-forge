# Definition

A **team** is a set of people organized to function cooperatively as a group. Five elements support team functioning:

- Common commitment and purpose.
- Specific performance goals.
- Complementary skills.
- Commitment to how the work gets done.
- Mutual accountability.

Teams perform real work together, share leadership roles, combine individual and mutual accountability, and assess collective work products.

# Team and Working Group

A **working group** typically has a focused leader, individual accountabilities and work products, and a purpose aligned with the broader organizational mission. A team has a more specific shared purpose and evaluates what members produce collectively.

# Development Stages

Tuckman's model describes five stages:

1. **Forming:** members are introduced, remain polite, and look to a leader for structure and direction.
2. **Storming:** members test group processes, compete for influence, and experience conflict over control, goals, and tasks.
3. **Norming:** members establish ground rules, share responsibility, and develop respect and commitment to group goals.
4. **Performing:** the team becomes largely self-directed and aligns its members, work, and vision around results.
5. **Adjourning:** the team disbands at the end of its project or purpose.

Development is not strictly linear. Membership changes, altered scope, or new tasks can return a team to an earlier stage.
