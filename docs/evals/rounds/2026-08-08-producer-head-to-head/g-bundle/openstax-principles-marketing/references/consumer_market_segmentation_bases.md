# Consumer Market Segmentation Bases

Market segmentation divides a market into groups of customers with relevant characteristics in common. Consumer markets are commonly segmented using four major bases.

| Segmentation base | Guiding question | Common variables |
|---|---|---|
| Geographic | Where are customers? | Country, region, county, postal code, climate, season, language, cultural preference, and urban/suburban/rural density. |
| Demographic | Who are customers? | Gender, income, age, life stage, generation, education, race, ethnicity, religion, occupation, marital status, and family structure. |
| Behavioral | How do customers interact with the offering? | Benefits sought, purchase occasion, usage level, loyalty, and other observed behavior patterns. Usage levels may be classified as non-user, light, medium, or heavy. |
| Psychographic | Why do customers buy? | Lifestyle, personality, and values. Lifestyle is often described through AIO variables: activities, interests, and opinions. |

Organizations may combine several bases when a single variable does not describe a useful segment precisely enough.
