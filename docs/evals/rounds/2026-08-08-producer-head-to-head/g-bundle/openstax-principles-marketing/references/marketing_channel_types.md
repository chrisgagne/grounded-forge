# Marketing Channel Types

A marketing channel is the system of people, organizations, and activities that makes goods and services available for use.

## Channel paths

| Type | Path and meaning |
|---|---|
| Direct channel | Producer → consumer or business user. The producer distributes the offering without an intermediary. |
| Indirect channel | Producer → one or more intermediaries → consumer or business user. Intermediaries may include agents, distributors, wholesalers, and retailers. |
| Producer–retailer–consumer | A consumer-market indirect channel in which the producer sells to a retailer without a wholesaler or distributor. |
| Producer–wholesaler–retailer–consumer | A consumer-market indirect channel in which a wholesaler aggregates and redistributes products before retailers sell them to final consumers. |
| Producer–industrial distributor–business user | A business-market indirect channel in which a distributor moves industrial products from producers to organizational customers. |

Agents facilitate negotiations but do not ordinarily take ownership of the product; distributors, wholesalers, and retailers can take ownership as products move through the channel.

## Marketing systems

| System | Definition |
|---|---|
| Vertical marketing system | Channel members coordinate as a unified, collaborative, customer-focused system rather than maximizing each member's result independently. |
| Horizontal marketing system | Unrelated organizations cooperate to offer products or services in a shared space or opportunity. |
| Multichannel marketing system | One company establishes multiple distribution channels to reach customers. |
| Omnichannel marketing system | A multichannel approach that gives customers integrated ways to purchase, receive, and return products as one seamless experience. |
