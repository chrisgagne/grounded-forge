# Marketing Mix

The marketing mix is the set of tactics an organization can use to promote a product or service and influence purchase. It is commonly organized as the **4Ps**.

| Element | Meaning | Typical decisions |
|---|---|---|
| Product | The good or service the organization provides. | Features, design, quality, branding, packaging, and which customer needs to satisfy. |
| Price | What the customer pays in exchange for the offering. | Price points, discounts, profitability, production costs, and perceived value. |
| Place | Where and how the customer obtains the offering. | Distribution channels, outlets, availability, fulfillment, and delivery to the customer's location. |
| Promotion | The communication used to attract customers and explain the offering's value. | Advertising, public relations, selling, digital communication, and other promotional methods. |

The four elements should be designed together for the selected target market rather than treated as isolated decisions.
