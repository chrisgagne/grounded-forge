# New Product Adopter Categories

Diffusion-of-innovation theory groups consumers by how early they adopt a new product or idea.

| Category | Adoption behavior |
|---|---|
| Innovators | Take risks and buy new products as soon as they reach the market, accepting the possibility of failure. |
| Early adopters | Adopt soon after innovators and often serve as opinion leaders whose recommendations influence others. |
| Early majority | Wait for evidence from earlier adopters but accept an innovation before the average person. |
| Late majority | Adopt only after an innovation is established and a substantial portion of the population already uses it; they favor simple, cost-effective offerings. |
| Laggards | Are wary of new ideas and adopt so late that a replacement or newer version may already exist. |

These categories describe relative adoption timing; they are not the same as psychographic segments that happen to use similar labels.
