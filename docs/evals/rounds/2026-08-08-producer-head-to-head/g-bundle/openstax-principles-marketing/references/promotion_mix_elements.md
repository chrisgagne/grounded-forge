# Promotion Mix Elements

The promotion mix is the set of strategies marketers use to communicate with customers. Organizations commonly combine its elements into a coordinated integrated marketing communications message.

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| Advertising | Paid, nonpersonal communication about a product, service, or idea from an identified source. |
| Sales promotion | A short-term incentive designed to produce an immediate action or increase in sales. |
| Personal selling | One-to-one communication in which a seller informs and persuades a buyer. |
| Public relations | Nonpaid, nonpersonal communication used to influence awareness, reputation, and relationships with publics. |
| Direct marketing | Communication that connects directly with a customer and generally seeks a response or transaction. |
| Internet/digital marketing | Promotion through Internet and digital technologies such as social media, applications, and text messaging. |

Using several elements with one consistent message increases the opportunities for the intended audience to receive and recognize that message.
