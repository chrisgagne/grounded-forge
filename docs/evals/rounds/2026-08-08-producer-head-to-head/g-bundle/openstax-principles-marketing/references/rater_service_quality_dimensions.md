# RATER Service Quality Dimensions

The RATER framework identifies five dimensions customers use when evaluating service quality.

| Dimension | Meaning | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| Reliability | The ability to provide accurate, dependable, and on-time service consistently. | Was the promised service delivered correctly? |
| Assurance | The degree to which the organization and its service providers inspire trust and confidence. | Did the provider demonstrate credible expertise and competence? |
| Tangibles | The physical appearance of facilities, equipment, materials, and employees. | Do the visible cues support the expected level of quality? |
| Empathy | Attentive, caring, and individualized treatment that makes customers feel valued. | Did the provider understand and care about the customer's situation? |
| Responsiveness | Promptness and willingness to help and provide satisfactory service. | Did the provider respond quickly and make a genuine effort to help? |
