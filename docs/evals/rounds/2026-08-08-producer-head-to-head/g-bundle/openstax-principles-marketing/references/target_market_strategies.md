# Target Market Strategies

A target market is the subset of the total market on which an organization focuses its marketing resources. Four generic strategies describe how many segments it serves and how specifically it adapts the marketing mix.

| Strategy | Also called | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Undifferentiated marketing | Mass marketing | Uses one marketing mix for the entire market, typically when meaningful customer differences are limited or broad appeal and scale are advantageous. |
| Differentiated marketing | Segmented marketing | Selects several target markets and develops a distinct strategy or offering for each. |
| Concentrated marketing | Niche marketing | Focuses one marketing mix on a single selected segment, concentrating resources and seeking a strong position within that niche. |
| Micromarketing | Customized marketing | Targets a specific group or individuals within a niche using detailed information about them. |

A **target market** is not necessarily the same as a **target audience**: the audience expected to make the purchase can be narrower or different from the users for whom the product is intended.
