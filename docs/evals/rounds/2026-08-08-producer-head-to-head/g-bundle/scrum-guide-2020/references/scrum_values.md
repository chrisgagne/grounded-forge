# Values

Successful use of Scrum depends on people becoming proficient in five values:

- **Commitment:** the Scrum Team commits to achieving its goals and supporting one another.
- **Focus:** the Scrum Team's primary focus is the work of the Sprint and the best possible progress toward its goals.
- **Openness:** the Scrum Team and its stakeholders are open about the work and its challenges.
- **Respect:** Scrum Team members respect one another as capable, independent people and receive the same respect from those with whom they work.
- **Courage:** Scrum Team members have the courage to do the right thing and work on difficult problems.

# Application

The values guide the Scrum Team's work, actions, and behavior. Decisions, steps taken, and the way Scrum is used should reinforce rather than undermine them. When the Scrum Team and the people around it embody these values, the empirical pillars of transparency, inspection, and adaptation support trust.
