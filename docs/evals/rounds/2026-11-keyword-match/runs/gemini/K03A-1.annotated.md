# K03A-1

Why should a learning review avoid counterfactual arguments?

A learning review (such as a Facilitated Learning Analysis) should avoid counterfactual arguments—statements such as "if only X had happened," "they failed to," or "they should have"—for several key reasons[1:IN:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024][2:OUT:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024]:
Counterfactuals are contrary to fact and inherently unknowable: Counterfactual reasoning wrongly presumes that reviewers can predict an alternate future[2:OUT:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024]. In reality, the individual involved did not take the hypothetical action, and it is impossible to know what would have occurred had they done so[3:OUT:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024].
They fail to explain why actions were taken: Explaining what individuals could have or should have done explains nothing about why they took the specific actions they did[4:OTHER:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][5:OTHER:doe-hpi-handbook-vol2]. A learning review is only effective when it uncovers why decisions and behaviors made sense to the people involved in real time, given their context and information at the moment[1:IN:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024][4:OTHER:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][5:OTHER:doe-hpi-handbook-vol2].
They are driven by hindsight and blind reviewers to context: Seductive counterfactuals stem from hindsight bias[2:OUT:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024][4:OTHER:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][5:OTHER:doe-hpi-handbook-vol2][6:OUT:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024]. Relying on them blinds the review team to the actual operational context, environmental conditions, goal conflicts, and systemic pressures present during the event[1:IN:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024][2:OUT:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024][4:OTHER:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1].
They distract from systemic learning: Counterfactual arguments shift the focus to individual omissions or perceived shortcomings rather than examining flaws in organizational processes, procedures, and system controls[1:IN:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024][2:OUT:lfuo-learning-review-guide-2024][4:OTHER:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1].
