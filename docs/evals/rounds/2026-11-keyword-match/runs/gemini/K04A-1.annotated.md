# K04A-1

What is the Definition of Done, and what happens to a Product Backlog item that doesn't meet it?

The Definition of Done (DoD) is a formal description of the state of the product Increment when it meets the quality measures required for the product[1:IN:scrum-guide-2020]. It serves as the official commitment for the Scrum Increment artifact, creating transparency by giving the Scrum Team and stakeholders a shared understanding of what work was completed[1:IN:scrum-guide-2020][2:OUT:scrum-guide-2020]. It encapsulates agreed-upon team criteria regarding quality, testing, assurance, and non-functional requirements that every item must satisfy[3:ALT:open-practice-library][4:OUT:scrum-guide-2020]. The exact moment a Product Backlog item satisfies the Definition of Done, an Increment is born[1:IN:scrum-guide-2020].
If a Product Backlog item does not meet the Definition of Done, the following rules apply:
It cannot be released: Work cannot be considered part of an Increment unless it meets the Definition of Done, meaning it cannot be delivered to users[1:IN:scrum-guide-2020][5:IN:scrum-guide-2020].
It cannot be presented at the Sprint Review: Incomplete items must not be shown or demonstrated during the Sprint Review, as presenting unfinished work can erode trust with stakeholders[1:IN:scrum-guide-2020][6:OTHER:approach-perfect-field-guide-scrum-events].
It returns to the Product Backlog: The item is sent back to the Product Backlog for future consideration and re-prioritization by the Product Owner and team[1:IN:scrum-guide-2020].
No partial credit is awarded: The team does not receive partial story points or incomplete work credit for the Sprint[7:OTHER:approach-perfect-field-guide-scrum-events].
