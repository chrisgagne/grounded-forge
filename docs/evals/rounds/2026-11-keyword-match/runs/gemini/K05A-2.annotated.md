# K05A-2

What is the scope of work mandate on the vertical axis of the Org Topologies map?

On the Org Topologies map, the vertical axis represents the Scope of Work Mandate assigned to organizational units[1:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025][2:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025]. This axis determines the size of a unit's "field of play," how units collaborate, and how easily an organization can pivot its strategic direction[1:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025][2:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Core Principles of the Vertical Axis
Collaboration and Field of Play: As a unit moves higher on the vertical axis, its scope of work expands[1:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025]. At the highest levels, work scopes between units overlap, encouraging them to co-own goals and operate as a cohesive "Team-of-Teams"[1:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Economic Switching Costs: Vertically higher positioning corresponds to lower switching costs when changing focus or adopting new strategic directions driven by market feedback, innovation, or competition[2:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Outputs vs. Outcomes: Units situated at lower vertical levels concentrate on generating outputs (specific tasks or features), whereas units at higher levels operate within a broader business context that aligns them directly with customer value and business outcomes[2:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025][3:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025][4:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025].
The 5 Vertical Levels (From Bottom to Top)
Tasks Focus: Units receive fine-grained, highly specific task assignments aligned tightly with narrow technical or functional expertise (e.g., sketching a single wireframe or chopping ingredients)[5:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Capabilities Focus: Units own a full capability, feature, or service element within a broader offering (e.g., an Engine Team or a Claims-Processing Group)[5:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Partial-Business Focus: Units focus on a distinct business vertical or profit center clearly separated from other divisions (e.g., Uber Ride versus Uber Eats), driving cross-team collaboration around business outcomes[6:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Whole-Business Focus: Units collaborate directly on whole-business problems without structural barriers, sub-product silos, or rigid vertical divisions[7:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Unbounded Focus: Units possess a mandate for open exploration, allowing them to pursue undiscovered customer needs in unknown markets and learn whatever skills are required (e.g., a startup pivoting to explore entirely new product avenues)[7:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
