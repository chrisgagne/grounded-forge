# K05B-2

Our squads get handed tiny tickets like "tweak this button". How do I describe how far up the ladder of responsibility a squad's remit reaches, from chores to an entire product?

To describe how far up the ladder of responsibility a squad's remit reaches, your sources—specifically the Org Topologies framework—provide a structured model called the Scope of Work Mandate (the vertical axis of strategic organizational design)[1:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025][2:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
This framework defines five vertical levels of responsibility that map a squad's remit from performing minor chores up to owning an entire product or business domain:
1. The 5 Levels of the Work Mandate Ladder
Tasks Focus (The Chores Level): The squad operates on fine-grained, narrow task inputs (e.g., "tweak this button" or "sketch a wireframe")[3:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025]. The squad acts as a Doing Archetype, relying on external planners, managers, or coordinators to define, decompose, and dispatch the work[3:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025][4:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025][5:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Capabilities Focus (The Feature Level): The squad owns a full capability or feature set within a product or service (e.g., a search engine team or claims-processing team)[3:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025]. They deliver functional components predictably, but their scope is restricted to their specific capability silo[3:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025][6:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Partial-Business Focus (The Sub-Product / Vertical Level): The squad operates within a distinct business vertical or profit center (e.g., Uber Ride vs. Uber Eats)[7:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025]. Teams at this level collaborate closely across boundaries because they are held accountable for real business outcomes and customer value rather than just output[7:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025][8:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Whole-Business Focus (The Entire Product Level): Squads collaborate on whole-business problems, overall product direction, and customer value without internal domain silos[9:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025][10:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025]. They function as Driving Archetypes, uniting vision, discovery, and execution into a single self-steering unit[10:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025][11:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Unbounded Focus (The Strategic Exploration Level): Squads have the mandate to explore undiscovered customer needs and unknown markets, pivoting freely to build brand-new value streams[9:IN:org-topologies-primer-2025].
2. Organizational Topologies: How Squad Remit Defines the Culture
Org Topologies groups these levels into three broader Topologies to diagnose how an organization assigns authority:
Resource Topology (Directing + Doing): Squads are treated as resource pools executing task-level tickets assigned by directing roles whose primary goal is 100% resource utilization[12:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025][13:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025][14:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Delivery Topology (Directing + Delivering): Squads are cross-functional "feature factories" optimized for shipping complete features quickly, though high-level product strategy and discovery remain managed from above[6:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025][15:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025].
Adaptive Topology (Driving): Squads own both the discovery and delivery of whole-product solutions, adapting their direction continuously based on real customer outcomes[11:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025][16:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025][17:OUT:org-topologies-primer-2025].
3. Complementary Product & Agile Perspective
In Scrum and product management practices, elevating a squad up this ladder requires shifting from managing low-level work items to giving the squad shared accountability for a Product Goal and a single Product Backlog[18:OTHER:scrum-guide-2020][19:OTHER:scrum-guide-2020][20:OTHER:scrum-guide-2020]. Leadership sets the overarching problem and goal, leaving room for the squad to leverage their expertise to figure out the "how" and deliver usable increments of value[19:OTHER:scrum-guide-2020][21:OTHER:approach-perfect-field-guide-scrum-events][22:OTHER:scrum-guide-2020].
