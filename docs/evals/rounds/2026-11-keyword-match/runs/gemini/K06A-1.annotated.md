# K06A-1

What is the paper plant, and how can flaws in it lead to error in the physical plant?

The physical plant consists of the actual systems, structures, and physical components used in a facility's production processes[1:IN:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][2:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1]. Front-line workers directly "touch" and manipulate the physical plant when executing their assigned tasks[1:IN:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][3:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1].
The paper plant refers to the aggregate of all documentation and directives that control and define the configuration and operation of the physical plant[1:IN:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][4:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1]. This includes procedures, instructions, engineering drawings, specifications, policies, work packages, and design bases documentation[1:IN:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][4:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][5:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1]. Knowledge workers—such as engineers, procedure writers, and technical staff—are responsible for creating and modifying the paper plant[1:IN:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][6:ALT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol2].
Flaws in the paper plant translate into physical errors through the following sequence:
Embedding Latent Conditions: When knowledge workers make incorrect calculations, leave instructions vague, omit critical steps, or fail to update drawings, these inaccuracies become embedded within the paper plant as latent errors[5:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][7:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][8:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][9:OTHER:caib-report-vol1].
Dormant Period: Unlike physical active errors, flaws in paper-based directives usually do not provide immediate feedback[5:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][7:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1]. They lie dormant in the documentation until an operational activity calls for that documentation to be used in the field[1:IN:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][5:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1].
Provoking Active Errors: When front-line workers execute a job using flawed, inaccurate, or ambiguous instructions, the paper flaw acts as an error trap[5:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][10:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][11:ALT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol2]. Even compliant workers following instructions as written can be led to perform the wrong action[11:ALT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol2][12:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1].
Physical Plant Alterations: As a result of executing the flawed paper instructions, workers unknowingly alter the physical plant state[5:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1]. This manifests as physical errors such as installing an incorrect gasket, mispositioning a valve, placing warning tags on wrong components, or invalidating the design bases of safety-related equipment—ultimately risking equipment damage, system failure, or personal injury[1:IN:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][5:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][7:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1][13:OUT:doe-hpi-handbook-vol1].
💡 We could explore the specific human performance tools—such as peer reviews, independent verifications, or technical task walkdowns—that help identify and eliminate latent flaws in paper directives before they reach the physical plant.
