What are the two axes of the Agreement/Uncertainty Matrix?

The two axes of the Agreement/Uncertainty Matrix (also referred to as the Stacey Matrix) are:
Degree of Agreement: Measures the level of consensus or alignment among stakeholders regarding what to do or the meaning of different aspects of the situation (ranging on the vertical axis from Close to Agreement to Far from Agreement)[1:OTHER:open-practice-library][2:IN:liberating-structures-handbook].
Degree of Certainty: Measures how well-understood, predictable, or certain the situation and its future outcomes are (ranging on the horizontal axis from Close to Certainty to Far from Certainty)[1:OTHER:open-practice-library][2:IN:liberating-structures-handbook].
By plotting a decision or situation along these two dimensions, teams can identify whether a task is simple, complicated, complex, or chaotic/anarchic and choose the most suitable management approach[1:OTHER:open-practice-library][2:IN:liberating-structures-handbook][3:OTHER:open-practice-library].
