# K10A-2

What are the two axes of the Agreement/Uncertainty Matrix?

The two axes of the Agreement/Uncertainty Matrix (also referred to as the Stacey Matrix) are:
Degree of Certainty (often plotted on the horizontal axis): Measures how certain or predictable the situation, cause-and-effect relationships, or future outcomes are[1:OTHER:open-practice-library][2:OTHER:open-practice-library][3:IN:liberating-structures-handbook].
Degree of Agreement (often plotted on the vertical axis): Measures the level of consensus or agreement among stakeholders and group members regarding what actions to take or how to interpret the situation[1:OTHER:open-practice-library][2:OTHER:open-practice-library][3:IN:liberating-structures-handbook].
By plotting a problem or decision along these two dimensions, organizations can distinguish whether a situation is simple, complicated, complex, or anarchic/chaotic, which helps leaders choose the appropriate management or decision-making strategy[1:OTHER:open-practice-library][2:OTHER:open-practice-library][3:IN:liberating-structures-handbook].
