grounded-forge

A workshop for grounded assistants: facilitators, coaches, analysts, advisors.

The library reads each source once, under audit, and projects it onto every task the assistant supports. Answers cite back to the passage they rest on, so a reader can check every claim.

Marine warfighting doctrine and the business textbook: what US Marine doctrine and textbook business administration have in common, answered from the open demo corpus in verbatim mode. Every quotation is placed from its source by code, and every claim opens its passage with the printed page highlighted and, where the passage names one, the source's own figure. About five minutes.
