What these controls do

Quotations kept. The share of quotations shown in the author's words, most important first. Below 100%, the rest show as the model's paraphrase of them.

The model's own sentences. Everything on the page that isn't an author's words or a paraphrase of a passage is the model's: the sentences that introduce a passage, link one to the next, and say what they mean for the question. Full prose reads as an essay. Bare links cuts those sentences to a few words, so nearly everything left is the sources.

Source shown around a quotation. Click any quotation or paraphrase and the margin shows the passage in the source text, highlighted. This sets how much of the text around it you see.

As it would ship. A distribution built to share carries only openly licensed wording, so quotations from copyrighted and private sources turn into the model's paraphrase.

Swap each paraphrase for the author's passage. Where the model wrote its own sentence about a passage (dashed underline, tagged paraphrase of a passage), show the passage itself in its place. Either way, a click opens the passage.
